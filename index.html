Dr. S. Tamil Selvan

Professor, Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering

I bridge two worlds that are usually kept apart: 21 years of total experience, split between 14 years of teaching across the ranks of Lecturer, Assistant Professor, Associate Professor and Professor — with a stint as Head of Department — and 7 years in the software industry as an iOS and Android developer, Product Manager, design engineer and full-stack developer. My work is centred on building engineering talent that can ship production software and understand the science underneath it.

Alongside both tracks I work as an AI trainer and developer using Claude, Cursor, Gemini and ChatGPT — running workshops for students, faculty and industry teams, and applying these tools to real engineering work. The emphasis throughout is verification: confident output still has to be checked.

AI trainer & developer

Practical AI for engineers and students

I work directly with the current generation of AI development tools, and I teach what actually works — including where these tools fail and how to verify their output.

🤖

Claude

Long-context reasoning over datasheets, standards and codebases. Useful for design review, verification strategy and HDL drafting.

✍

Cursor

AI-native editor for refactoring, legacy code comprehension and test scaffolding inside a real development workflow.

❄

Gemini

Multimodal analysis and large-context research support, including document, diagram and dataset reasoning.

ChatGPT

Everyday assistant for explanation, rapid prototyping, structured learning material and iterative problem solving.

Training

Workshops for students

Hands-on sessions on using AI assistants for VLSI and software work — Verilog and HDL generation, simulation script writing, debugging, literature summarisation and test case development, with emphasis on verifying every output.

Training

Workshops for faculty

Practical sessions for teaching staff on using AI to prepare laboratory manuals, design assignment questions, generate question banks and build documentation, while keeping academic integrity and assessment standards intact.

Development

AI-assisted engineering

Applying these tools to real delivery: legacy code understanding, automated refactoring, test generation and documentation. My seven years in industry — including code review and release discipline — is what makes this trustworthy rather than a demo.

The principle I teach: AI assistants are fast junior engineers, not authorities. The value is in knowing precisely what to ask and being able to check the answer. A student who cannot verify a generated Verilog module has learned nothing.
